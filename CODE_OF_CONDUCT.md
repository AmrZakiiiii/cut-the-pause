# Code of Conduct

This project follows the Contributor Covenant 2.1.

## Our Standards

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent, but be direct about technical problems.
- Focus on the work, not the person.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment or discrimination.
- Personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without consent.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, issues, or contributions that violate these standards and may restrict participation for repeated or severe violations.
